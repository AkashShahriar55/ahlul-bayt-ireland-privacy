Privacy Policy

Ahlul Bayt Ireland app · Effective 25 October 2026

This policy explains what the Ahlul Bayt Ireland app (Android package ie.ahlulbayt.ahlul_bayt_ireland, and the Ahlul Bayt Ireland web app) does with your information. In short: there are no ads, no accounts for members, no tracking and no selling of data. We only use what the app needs to work.

"We" means Ahlul Bayt Ireland, the Shia Muslim community centre in Dublin, Ireland. You can reach us at contact.ahlulbaytireland@gmail.com.

What the app collects

DataWhenWhy
Your location (approximate or precise) Only if you allow location access, to show prayer times for where you are Your coordinates are sent to the AlAdhan prayer-time service to calculate the times. We do not store your location. If you say no, the app uses Dublin.
An install ID Created at random the first time you open the app Sent with feedback, quiz scores and prayer-time reports so we can spot duplicates and abuse. It is not linked to your name, phone number or advertising ID.
Feedback and problem reports Only when you send them The message you write, its category, the platform (Android or web) and, if you choose to give it, a contact email so we can reply.
Prayer-time reports Only when you report a wrong prayer time The prayer, the time shown, the time you expected, the location name and any note, so we can correct the timetable.
Quiz display name and score Only when you submit a quiz score Shown on the public leaderboard together with your score. Use a nickname rather than your full name.

Settings such as language, text size, adhan choice and bookmarks stay on your device and are never sent to us.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising and no advertising IDs.
  • No analytics or tracking tools.
  • No member accounts or passwords. (A separate sign-in exists only for the volunteer who edits the app's content.)
  • We never sell your data or share it for marketing.

Services the app uses

To work, the app contacts these services. Each receives your device's IP address as part of a normal internet request:

  • Supabase: hosts the app's content and stores the feedback, reports and quiz scores described above.
  • AlAdhan (aladhan.com): calculates prayer times from your location.
  • Unsplash: provides the wallpaper images.
  • YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode, youtube-nocookie.com): plays videos you choose to open.
  • Google Play: delivers the app and updates under Google's privacy policy.

Notifications

In the Android app, adhan and reminder notifications are scheduled on your phone, and no notification data is sent to us. Turn them off in the app or in Android settings.

In the web app, if you allow notifications, your browser gives us a push subscription: an anonymous address that lets us send community announcements to that browser. It is stored in Supabase and deleted when you turn notifications off or the subscription expires.

Security

All data the app sends travels over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

How long we keep it

Feedback and prayer-time reports are kept until the issue is dealt with and for at most 12 months. Quiz scores stay on the leaderboard until you ask us to remove them. Location is never stored.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you, or object to its use. Email contact.ahlulbaytireland@gmail.com. Because the app has no accounts, tell us roughly when you sent the feedback or your quiz nickname so we can find it. We reply within 30 days. You can also complain to Ireland's Data Protection Commission.

We use your data on the basis of our legitimate interest in running the app for the community, and on your consent where you choose to share location or send something to us.

Children

Kids Corner is meant to be used by children with their families. The app does not ask children for personal information. If a child has sent us something, such as a quiz name, a parent can ask us to delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
